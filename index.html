Who we are

Founded in 2006 by students from oil & gas, utilities, and outside the sector entirely, we've kept that mix at the heart of our culture ever since. Some of us arrive as engineers; most arrive curious.

Energy is one of the most prominent industries going through a major transformation — and it's wide open to MBA profiles.

Our mission is to develop the next generation of energy leaders at IESE through career support, hands-on learning, and real connections with the people who run the industry.

What we do

Three things, done well.

Our priorities for the year map to how members actually get value: understand the sector, meet the right people, and land the role.

Learn

Knowledge sessions and hands-on learning that make a fast-changing, complex sector make sense. No prior energy background required.

Connect

Meet students, alumni, and industry practitioners who share a serious interest in the energy transition — on campus and off.

Recruit

On- and off-campus recruiting support, treks, and company visits to help you land internships and full-time roles.
